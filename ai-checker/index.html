How this works

The checkers run inside this browser. Your text is not sent anywhere when you press Check. Rewrites are written by Claude through your own Claude account.

Universities mostly use Turnitin, GPTZero and Copyleaks (DrillBit through ShodhShuddhi in India). None of them publish their models, so they cannot run here. These are the strongest open-source detectors on the RAID benchmark (University of Pennsylvania) that fit in a browser.

Scores are calibrated on 599 human-written samples (news, essays, Reddit posts, abstracts and more). "Flags AI" means the answer reads as more AI-like than 95% of those samples. To cross-check with a tool universities use, paste the final text into GPTZero.
