How this works

Every checker runs inside this browser. Your text is not uploaded when you press Check, and rewrites made with the local or in-browser engine stay on your computer.

Universities mostly use Turnitin, GPTZero and Copyleaks (DrillBit through ShodhShuddhi in India). Turnitin and DrillBit cannot be called by individuals, so nothing here can reproduce their verdict exactly. These are the strongest open-source detectors that fit in a browser, from different training sets. On 300 held-out articles they agreed with GPTZero and Pangram on about 92% of verdicts.

Their blind spot, measured: answers written plainly by the newest models. Of 29 test answers on Indian lending written by a current Claude model, only about 1 in 7 was flagged and 1 in 4 rated Medium or High. (On the held-out articles GPTZero caught 84% of the AI ones and Pangram 98%.) So treat Low as "these checkers did not flag it", not as a pass, and use the GPTZero or Pangram buttons for a second opinion on anything that matters.

Scores are compared with professional human writing. "Flags AI" means more AI-like than 95% of that writing.

No rewrite method guarantees a pass. Since August 2026 Turnitin counts text changed by "bypasser" tools as AI, so the safest changes are your own: a real example, the actual number, your own view. If your programme does not allow AI help, using an AI rewriter may break its rules whatever the scores say.

Rewrite flagged sentences

Each sentence gets several versions. A version is thrown out if it drops or changes a number, name, negation or "may/will", says something different (checked by a small meaning model), or adds a grammar slip. Of the rest, the one the checkers rate most human is kept. One checker is held back and only used to confirm the result.

Open Claude in a new tab

Set up the local rewriter (Humanizer 12B)
