# Style guide (calibrated)

All HARD RULES in style_brief.md apply.

## a. Voice
Karan is a capable 24-year-old talking plainly to a faculty member he respects. He states what he did with its real scope, and what he has not done as a flat fact ("What I have not done is take a project from an idea to a fully financed production"). Warmth comes from short true asides about people ("because nobody else was going to"), not adjectives or lessons. Images come only from real nouns in his work: a call sheet, a PPM deck, the potters' photographs. Every answer ends on a fact, a plan or a next step.

## b. DO
- One gap per answer: what he can do, what he has not done, the programme feature that covers it.
- Keep the source's concrete nouns and names (Nitin Rai, Suncity MUN, Pankaj Kumar).
- Make values personal (pair 5 below).
- Treat each university (USC 03/04/05, Chapman 06/07, LMU 08/09, NYU 13) as one package: the no-film-family fact once, the studio-for-people-without-connections note once (in the career-goal answer if one exists), the gap worded differently each time.
- "I plan to" for the studio; "will" only for near-term steps.
- A moved fact keeps its source verb.
- 2,000-character fields: 1,250 to 1,750.

## b. DON'T
- Maxim or moral-tag closers ("A shoot works the same way.").
- "So I learned to..." chains; summary topic sentences.
- Ordinals, rhythm tricolons, runs of short antithetical sentences.
- Jargon for a picture ("from development through release").
- Telling the school how it fits him.
- Echoing a dramatic prompt word; ranking experiences.

## c. Blacklist (rotate the alternatives)
- "frugal with money that was not mine": careful with money; kept a record of every rupee; treated shared money as a trust.
- Door images, "real responsibility": many first jobs come through family; I had no clear way in; trained, paid fairly and trusted with responsibility.
- "the whole pipeline": development, financing and distribution; how a project is found, financed and sold.
- "a budget line nobody owned", "a schedule built on hope", "a location that fell through": the bracketed example plus one item, once per university: a cost no one was in charge of; a location never confirmed.
- "people at the edges": the newest person in the room; the crew.
- "hold things together for others with little power": USC Production item 1 only; elsewhere name the story itself.
- "could not give that community everything it needed": could not undo what the lockdown had done; kept doing the part we could do.
- "people I had only just met": families who depended on us.
- "the photograph was the sale": buyers saw the pots through my photographs; a picture could decide what a family earned.
- "These jobs taught me how a shoot runs": So I can plan a shoot and run one; my experience so far is in physical production.
- "a plan only works when everyone trusts each other": we trusted each other to cover our parts; nobody waits to be told.
- "recognisable anywhere", bare "can travel": about work, family and who gets seen; can travel between India and the United States.
- Prose "glass towers", "learned by doing": the city was being built; learned on the job.

## d. Before / after
1. "In Indian film the first door is often opened by family." → "In Indian film many people get their start through family."
2. "I learned three things there." → "That work made me careful with money... so I kept a record of where each one went."
3. "calm on a set comes from clarity" → "My part was to make sure each decision was made, written down and shared."
4. "break the wall into steps" → "I work out what I am missing and go after it. What I am missing now is development and financing."
5. "respect for the people at the edges" → "On a set I notice the newest person in the room, because on my early jobs that was me."

## e. Item types
- Fiction (treatments, scenes, screenplays, stories): prose only. Keep plot, names, stakes and format; cut purple lines and explained feelings. If clean, leave it and say so.
- Video talking points: bullets, not a script; contractions fine; one idea and one real noun per bullet.
- Short fields (1,000 characters or less): two to five sentences; answer in the first; no closer.
- Lists, CV and entries: inventory only; keep format; dates, names, tools; no adjectives or lessons.

## f. Regression list (protect)
- "because nobody else was going to", "I did not have a word for that part of the work yet", "friends who were willing to give up their weekends", "how much one early chance can matter".
- "from the first draft of a script to the week a film reaches its audience"; "what a project can and cannot do for the people it touches".
- Khauf duties; the Finolex PPM deck; Sleepyhead as his first whole shoot; production jobs inside the B.Sc. ("Over the same years").
- Each school's named courses and thesis; a personal hook, not a CV line, opening every "about you" answer; warmth toward the potters' families, friends and crew.
