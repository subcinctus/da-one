# Notes
**Intake and deadline.** The MFA Producing starts in January, and the January 2027 round is already closed ("Applications are now closed for 2027 entry"). That leaves January 2028 as Karan's entry point, a year after his other programmes. NFTS has not yet published the 2028 closing date. A third-party listing, not NFTS, puts the close of the last round in late April 2026, so Karan should plan for a deadline around April 2027. Shortlisted applicants are interviewed, in person or on Zoom for overseas applicants, and successful interviewees then attend a five-day selection workshop, which costs £150.
**Eligibility.** NFTS looks for a passion for the subject and some evidence of it. Applicants normally hold an undergraduate degree, but NFTS also considers applicants without one if they have 3 to 4 years of relevant experience. Karan's B.Sc. awarded by the Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS) meets the usual route, so he does not need the alternative. For English, applicants from non-English-speaking countries must pass a Secure English Language Test (SELT) from an approved provider at CEFR level B2 or above. The admissions policy accepts IELTS for UKVI (Academic) at 6.5 overall with 6.5 in each component, and English is also assessed at interview.
**Recognition.** NFTS has been on The Hollywood Reporter's annual list of the best international film schools every year since the list began, 16 years in a row up to the 2026 edition, according to NFTS's own announcements.
**Before submitting.** For Producing, NFTS gives applicants a templated Word document in place of a portfolio. It asks for a 1,000-word project idea based on existing source material, up to 250 words on a favourite film or TV drama, and 75 words on each of two British producers or HETV executives. The online form also asks for his reasons for applying, his education and experience, interview availability and an equal opportunities questionnaire. Karan should line up one academic referee and one character referee early, and book the IELTS for UKVI test in time for the certificate to arrive before the deadline. The 2028 form may change, so check each item against the official NFTS page when it opens.
**Programme details** in the answers were taken from the school's official pages in October 2026; check each prompt word for word in the portal before submitting.
**Referees and English.** NFTS asks for two referees, one academic (a member of the Whistling Woods faculty who knows his cinematography training and student-set work) and one who has seen how he works on set, such as a senior member of a camera team. International applicants need a Secure English Language Test at CEFR B2; NFTS's admissions policy accepts IELTS for UKVI (Academic) at 6.5 overall with 6.5 in each component, so the test should be booked early enough for the result to arrive before the deadline.
