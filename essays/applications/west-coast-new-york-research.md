## A. Conditional add #1: Stony Brook University, MFA in Film (Producer track), Manhattan

- **Recognition**
  - It is not on The Hollywood Reporter's top-25 U.S. list; no search placed it there.
  - Its standing comes from industry ties. Christine Vachon (Killer Films) is artistic director, and Pamela Koffler teaches as visiting faculty. Students take "The Master Class in Independent Film Production with Vachon and Koffler."
  - The programme grew out of Vachon's master class, brought to SBU in 2012.
  - The programme's press page links Variety coverage of Vachon and Koffler.
  - It is a SUNY flagship and an R1 public university.
  - Sources: https://www.stonybrook.edu/commcms/mfa-film-tv/about/history.php , https://www.stonybrook.edu/commcms/mfa-film-tv/news/press-coverage.php
- **Producing focus**
  - It is a named specialization. The application text reads: "Your specialization: Director/Screenwriter/Producer/Independent Track (add to bottom of personal statement)."
  - The producing track "culminates in a feature producer's package, which includes serving as producer on a minimum of three films."
  - Programme directors told filmschool.org that producers leave with "a portfolio of at least 6 films" and must take sole producer credit on their thesis. That is secondary and about two years old.
  - Sources: https://catalog.stonybrook.edu/preview_program.php?catoid=10&poid=1175 , https://www.filmschool.org/articles/how-to-get-into-stony-brook-university-exclusive-advice-from-the-mfa-program-directors-part-1.204/
- **Fall 2027 deadline**
  - The programme admissions page shows a **scholarship deadline of 1/15/2027**. Last cycle that date applied to "both domestic and international applicants."
  - The programme says it "accepts applications for admission on a semi-rolling basis."
  - The previous cycle listed a **priority deadline of 15 March 2026** for domestic and international applicants.
  - Treat **15 Jan 2027** as the real target, since scholarships matter and international applicants need I-20 time. That is 96 days away and passes the 7-day rule.
  - Sources: https://www.stonybrook.edu/commcms/mfa-film-tv/admissions.php , https://www.stonybrook.edu/commcms/mfa-film-tv/about/
- **Eligibility for a 3-year Indian degree (official Graduate School text)**
  - "Three-year Bachelor's degrees from India... are reviewed case by case and must come from an institution with a NAAC grade of A or higher, graduating within first division or higher. The Graduate School notifies students if they must add a year through a postgraduate diploma or a Master's degree."
  - "A U.S. bachelor's degree or the equivalent is required (for example, a four-year/120 credit hour accredited program)."
  - Minimum undergraduate GPA is 3.0. IELTS 6.5 is required (or TOEFL iBT ~80, per aggregators).
  - **Karan:** TISS passes the NAAC test. **A 5.9/10 CGPA is almost certainly below "first division"**, which is usually 60% or 6.0 or more. As written, he would likely be told to add a year. Get written confirmation before investing in the application.
  - Sources: https://www.stonybrook.edu/grad/admissions/apply-graduate-programs.html , https://catalog.stonybrook.edu/content.php?catoid=10&navoid=915
- **Application components and prompts (catalog and programme)**
  - Résumé.
  - Three letters of recommendation.
  - Statement of purpose: "a page or two" on why the programme interests him, what he expects to gain, and why he stands out. He adds his specialization at the bottom.
  - **Video pitch**, about 3 minutes, uploaded in the digital portfolio section: "you can pitch to us anything you want: yourself as a candidate, a project you want to work on, or something else that is exciting to you." The catalog calls it "creative, polished, inventive" and "one of the more important elements to your application."
  - Producers do not submit a directing video sample: "Directing candidates only (not required for Screenwriter, Television Writer, or Producer candidates)."
  - **Producer writing sample**, up to 10 pages: "including an excerpt of a feature screenplay critiques, production program notes, and/or literary criticism of a chosen screenplay, with the option of a brief statement describing your view of the role of Produc[er]." The source text is truncated, so read the full wording on the portal.
  - Finalists may be interviewed.
  - Cost is roughly $50,000 for the full degree for out-of-state and international students, per the programme admissions page.

## B. Conditional add #2 (lower priority): Brooklyn College, Feirstein Graduate School of Cinema, MFA Cinema Arts (Live Action Filmmaking, producer thesis)

- **Recognition**
  - It has been on The Hollywood Reporter's top U.S. film schools list for **six consecutive years**. It entered at No. 25 in 2021 and was **No. 20 in the 2025 list**; the rank is from a secondary reproduction, so verify it on THR.
  - It is also on MovieMaker's 2026 Best Film Schools list.
  - Sources: https://www.brooklyn.edu/bc-news/hollywood-reporter-again-names-brooklyn-colleges-feirstein-graduate-school-of-cinema-one-of-the-best-in-the-u-s/ , https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/lists/best-film-schools-2026-u-s/ , https://www.brooklyn.edu/bc-news/feirstein-graduate-school-of-cinema-earns-spot-on-moviemakers-2026-best-film-schools-list/
- **Is the Producing concentration still offered for 2027? Apparently not as a stand-alone concentration.**
  - The current official programme page describes "a concentration in one of the following areas: Live Action Filmmaking or Digital Animation and Visual Effects." The old wording was "the two-year 52-credit concentration in producing." It survives only on older catalog and bursar pages.
  - A CUNY academic-program action from October 2023 describes the simplification as building on the directing concentration while **discontinuing producing, screenwriting and cinematography**.
  - What remains is a producer route inside Live Action: "To complete a thesis as a producer, students are required to take the following: 7274G, 7964G, 7953G." The course titles were not retrievable.
  - Students "direct projects from their first semester" and support peers "as cinematographers, producers, editors."
  - **Verdict:** this is a producing thesis option, not a dedicated producing degree, and it runs 3 years rather than the old 2. It meets his rule only loosely.
  - Sources: https://www.brooklyn.edu/academics/programs/cinema-arts-mfa/ , https://www.cuny.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/page-assets/academics/academic-programs/academic-program-resources/academic-reports/fall-2023-and-spring-2024/Brooklyn-College-October-2023-AURD.pdf
- **Fall 2027 deadline**
  - "Fall Application Deadline—January 15." No separate international date was published. Brooklyn "encourages international students to apply as early as possible."
  - Target: **15 Jan 2027**, which passes the 7-day rule.
  - Sources: https://www.brooklyn.edu/feirstein/admissions/ , https://www.brooklyn.edu/academics/programs/cinema-arts-mfa/
- **Eligibility (official text)**
  - "Applicants who have completed a bachelor's degree with a minimum GPA of 3.00 satisfy the undergraduate requirements of this program."
  - "Applicants with international credits must have the equivalent of a U.S. bachelor's degree."
  - Applicants must submit a course-by-course evaluation "from an NACES-accredited agency" showing "the US equivalent of a bachelor's degree."
  - F-1 applicants need TOEFL 79, IELTS 6.5, PTE 58-63 or Duolingo 105-160. Indian-medium exemptions are not stated.
  - **Karan:** WES treats a 3-year Indian degree as a US bachelor's only when it was "earned in Division I and the awarding institution holds an NAAC grade of 'A' or better." Otherwise it counts as "three years of undergraduate study." At 5.9/10 he likely fails the Division I condition.
  - Sources: https://brooklyn-graduate-preview.catalog.cuny.edu/graduate-admission , https://www.brooklyn.edu/admissions-aid/graduate/how-to-apply/faq/ , https://www.wes.org/resource-library/blog/credential-advice/3-year-indian-bachelors-degree/
- **Application components and prompts (official programme page)**
  - "Statement of purpose. Write a three- to four-page (double-spaced, 12-point font) statement explaining your purpose in undertaking graduate study in cinema arts."
  - Portfolio: one of two options.
    - "A short film that you have personally directed, or for which you were a principal creative contributor and credited as such (e.g., as writer, producer, cinematographer, editor, vfx designer/supervisor), not to exceed 15 minutes."
    - "An original treatment for a feature film, in 10 pages or less (double-spaced, 12-point font)," plus one page on why he wants to tell that story.
  - Two letters of recommendation, a résumé or CV, transcripts with the NACES evaluation, and English proficiency.
  - The fee is about $125 per aggregators; check it.
