# Notes
**Deadline.** Applications for the MA Film Producing open in December 2026 for the September 2027 intake. LFS has one application period and deadline for each September intake, but it has not yet published the closing date and says Home and International applicants may have different closing dates. Karan should check the deadlines page as soon as applications open. LFS says it interviews everyone who submits the full application pack, and applicants should expect to discuss their materials at the interview.
**Eligibility.** The entry requirement is a bachelor's degree with Honours, an equivalent international or higher qualification, or a relevant professional qualification. Karan's 3-year degree from the Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS) is not an Honours degree in the UK sense, so whether LFS treats it as equivalent is still unconfirmed. No minimum grade was found, so nothing found rules out his 5.9/10 CGPA. Selection puts weight on commitment to a producing career in feature films or high-end television drama, interest in the business side as well as the creative side, and relevant production experience, so his application should make these clear. For English, student-visa applicants whose first language is not English must take a Secure English Language Test (SELT), such as IELTS for UKVI. No score for Producing was found on the LFS site. The MA Filmmaking asks for 6.5 overall with no band below 6.0, and he should aim for that. A third-party listing gives 6.0 overall with no band below 5.5 for Producing, but this is unverified.
**Before submitting.** The application has five items: a treatment or synopsis of up to 400 words for a feature or drama series he would like to produce; a short CV; up to 200 words on why he would make a good producer; one 250-word answer chosen from three options; and his degree certificates. LFS has not published the 2027 wording, so these come from the current page, and only the online-resource option was confirmed word for word. Karan should check all the prompts against the live form once it opens in December. No work samples or recommendations appear in the confirmed list, but this is not certain for 2027, so he should gather his films and stills now, with his role noted on each, and ask his referees early. The films and stills will also help at the interview.
**Cost.** The application fee is £50 and is non-refundable. International tuition was given as £26,041, including a £300 visa admin fee, but this figure was not re-checked on LFS's fees page and should be confirmed before Karan budgets for it.
