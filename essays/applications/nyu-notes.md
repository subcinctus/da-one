# Notes
**Deadline.** The Tisch Graduate Film deadline is 1 December 2026; the Stern dual-degree deadline is 15 December 2026.
**Eligibility.** Tisch asks for a four-year degree, or three years plus a one-year postgraduate diploma, confirmed by a WES or ECE credential evaluation. Karan's degree is awarded by the Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS), and Tisch is likely out of reach unless the evaluation rates it as equivalent to four years.
**The Stern MBA.** Stern accepts three-year Indian degrees, but it considers dual-degree applicants only if Tisch invites them to interview. It also requires the GMAT or GRE.
